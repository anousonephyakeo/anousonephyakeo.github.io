About

I'm a Lao security engineer specialising in web, API and mobile penetration testing, AI red teaming, and security governance. I started as a developer at Lailaolab ICT Solutions in Vientiane, moved into offensive security, and today lead security testing work across finance and enterprise environments.

Alongside the technical work, I research how small businesses in developing economies can adopt security governance. My bachelor's thesis, Cybersecurity Governance in Lao e-Business SMEs, is one of the few academic studies on the topic in Laos.

I'm based in Budapest, studying Business Administration at MATE University, and I speak Lao, Thai and English.
